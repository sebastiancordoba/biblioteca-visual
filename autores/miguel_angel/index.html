Escultor, pintor y arquitecto florentino

Miguel Ángel Buonarroti

1475–1564

Nacido en Caprese el 6 de marzo de 1475, entró a los trece años en el taller de Domenico Ghirlandaio y tuvo por protector a Lorenzo de' Medici 1. Se tenía por escultor antes que pintor 1. En 1505 Julio II le encargó su tumba monumental; recortados los fondos, el papa lo puso a pintar la bóveda de la Sixtina 12. Firmó el contrato el 8 de mayo de 1508 y la terminó en 1512 32; trabajó de pie sobre un andamio, con el cuello doblado hacia atrás, no tumbado 4. Nueve escenas del Génesis, profetas, sibilas y desnudos se ordenan dentro de una arquitectura pintada 32. Murió en Roma el 18 de febrero de 1564 12.

Nota biográfica del Getty

In his time, Michelangelo was famous as a sculptor, painter, architect, and poet. He is remembered today as one of the chief creators of the High Renaissance in Florence and Rome, particularly in the arenas of sculpture and fresco painting. His professional career lasted over 70 years and included many stylistic changes. He was enormously influential to other artists. Michelangelo's father was the administrator of the small Florentine dependent town, Caprese. In Florence at age 13 (which was relatively late), Michelangelo became an apprentice to the city's most prominent painter, Domenico Ghirlandaio. His patron was the leading citizen and ruler of Florence, Lorenzo de' Medici. Michelangelo was typical of his period in that he excelled at and regarded the three arts of painting, sculpture, and architecture as manifestations of the fundamental discipline of "disegno" (drawing), which was itself based on a profound knowledge of the male human form.
